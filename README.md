# Dynamic Email Alias - Intelligent Custom Email Aliases

**Tagline:**
Instantly generate and autofill custom email aliases for every website you visit.

## Short Description
Protect your privacy and organize your inbox by generating unique email aliases (like `amazon.co.uk@yourdomain.com`) directly from the right-click menu.

---

## Full Description

Tired of giving out your primary email address to every newsletter and service? **Dynamic Email Alias** is the ultimate privacy and productivity tool for custom domain users.

Dynamic Email Alias automatically detects the website you are visiting and crafts a unique, professional email alias based on your custom domain. With a simple right-click, you can generate and autofill addresses like `netflix@yourdomain.com` or `linkedin@yourdomain.com`.

### Why use Dynamic Email Alias?
*   **Boost Privacy**: Trace exactly who shared or sold your email address. If you start getting spam at `adobe@yourdomain.com`, you know exactly where it came from.
*   **Stay Organized**: Filter incoming emails effortlessly based on the "To" address.
*   **Save Time**: No more typing out long addresses or thinking of new aliases.

### Key Features
*   ✨ **Smart Generation**: Automatically extracts the website name (including localized TLDs like `.co.uk`) to create readable aliases.
*   🚀 **One-Click Autofill**: Right-click any email field and select "Generate Email Alias".
*   🌐 **Multi-Domain Support**: Manage multiple custom domains and choose which one to use on the fly.
*   🔒 **Secure**: Runs entirely locally on your browser. No data is sent to external servers.

### How to use
1.  Install the extension.
2.  Open **Options** to add your custom domain (e.g., `my-privacy.net`).
3.  Right-click any email input field on the web.
4.  Select **Generate Email Alias**.
5.  Done! The field is instantly filled with `websitename@my-privacy.net`.
